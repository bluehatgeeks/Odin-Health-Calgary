# Odin Labs Calgary: Personnel Onboarding Guide

## 🚀 Welcome to Odin Labs
Our mission is to integrate ancient Ayurvedic wisdom with modern functional health technology. This guide ensures every new team member is aligned with our "Digital Glue" operations and clinical standards.

## 🛠 Technical Setup
All staff must be provisioned with the following:
- **Google Workspace**: Access to `Odin Labs Clinic Operations` shared drive.
- **GHL (GoHighLevel)**: Access to the patient pipeline and appointment calendar.
- **Internal Dashboard**: Access to the `Odin_Labs_Clinic_Dashboard` Google Sheet.

## 📋 Role-Specific Onboarding
### 1. Ayurvedic Practitioner
- [ ] Review `AYURVEDA_ROADMAP_TEMPLATE.md`.
- [ ] Sync with Lead on Prakriti assessment standards.
- [ ] Familiarize with the herbal sourcing inventory.

### 2. Health Coach
- [ ] Study the `Functional Health Blueprint`.
- [ ] Review patient habit-tracking templates.
- [ ] Training on metabolic audit hooks for high-net-worth clients.

### 3. Treatment Technician
- [ ] Hardware Safety Certification (PEMF, Hydrogen Inhaler).
- [ ] Marma Point protocol review.
- [ ] Daily equipment calibration checklist training.

### 4. Clinic Coordinator
- [ ] GHL Pipeline management training (Lead $\rightarrow$ Consult $\rightarrow$ Patient).
- [ ] Google Calendar synchronization for multi-modality sessions.
- [ ] Patient intake form auditing process.

## ⚖️ Clinical Standards
- **Privacy**: Strict adherence to HIPAA/Canadian health privacy laws.
- **Documentation**: All treatment logs must be updated in the `Treatment Protocol Log` immediately after the session.
- **Feedback Loop**: Weekly "Clinic Pulse" meeting to refine treatment protocols.

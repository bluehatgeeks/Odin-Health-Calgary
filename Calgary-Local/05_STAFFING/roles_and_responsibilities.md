# Odin Labs Calgary: Clinical Role Definitions

## 1. Ayurvedic Practitioner (Lead)
- **Core Responsibility**: Patient assessment (Prakriti/Vikriti), treatment plan design, and herbal prescription.
- **Key KPIs**: Patient adherence rate, symptom improvement score.
- **Onboarding Requirement**: Certification in Ayurvedic Medicine, alignment with Odin Labs holistic protocols.

## 2. Health Coach (Functional/Metabolic)
- **Core Responsibility**: Guiding patients through the 'Functional Health Blueprint', habit tracking, and dietary implementation.
- **Key KPIs**: Milestone completion rate, patient retention.
- **Onboarding Requirement**: Certification in Functional Nutrition or Health Coaching.

## 3. Treatment Technician (PEMF/Hydrogen/Marma)
- **Core Responsibility**: Safe operation of medical hardware (PEMF, Hydrogen Inhalers) and performing Marma therapy.
- **Key KPIs**: Equipment uptime, treatment session accuracy.
- **Onboarding Requirement**: Technical training on Odin Labs hardware and basic anatomy.

## 4. Clinic Coordinator (Patient Experience)
- **Core Responsibility**: Pipeline management, scheduling, and initial patient intake.
- **Key KPIs**: Lead-to-patient conversion rate, scheduling efficiency.
- **Onboarding Requirement**: Proficiency in GHL and Google Workspace.

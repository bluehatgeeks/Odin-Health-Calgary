# Odin Labs Calgary: Clinical Quality Assurance (CQA) Framework

## 🎯 Objective
Ensure consistency in treatment delivery across all modalities (Ayurveda, PEMF, Hydrogen, Health Coaching) to maintain the "Odin Standard" of care.

## 🔍 Audit Pillars
### 1. Protocol Adherence
- **Metric**: % of sessions where the `Treatment Protocol Log` matches the prescribed `Ayurvedic Roadmap`.
- **Audit Method**: Bi-weekly random sampling of 5 patient logs per practitioner.
- **Red Flag**: Modification of herbal dosage without a documented clinical review.

### 2. Equipment Calibration
- **Metric**: 100% uptime and calibration accuracy for PEMF and Hydrogen devices.
- **Audit Method**: Daily "Start-of-Day" checklist verification.
- **Red Flag**: Missing calibration logs for >24 hours.

### 3. Patient Outcome Tracking
- **Metric**: Subjective Improvement Score (SIS) trend per patient.
- **Audit Method**: Analysis of pre- and post-treatment surveys in the `Odin_Labs_Clinic_Dashboard`.
- **Red Flag**: Three consecutive sessions with zero reported improvement.

## 🛠 Remediation Workflow
`Audit Failure` $\rightarrow$ `Root Cause Analysis (RCA)` $\rightarrow$ `Staff Retraining` $\rightarrow$ `Re-Audit` $\rightarrow$ `Sign-off`.

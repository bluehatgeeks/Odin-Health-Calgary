# Odin Labs Calgary: Emergency & Incident Response Protocol (EIRP)

## 🎯 Objective
Provide clear, immediate action steps for clinical and operational emergencies to ensure patient safety and facility security.

## 🚨 Clinical Emergencies
### 1. Adverse Reaction (Herbal/Treatment)
- **Immediate Action**: Cease treatment immediately. Move patient to a recovery position.
- **Assessment**: Check vitals (Pulse, Respiration). 
- **Escalation**: If symptoms include anaphylaxis or loss of consciousness, call 911 immediately.
- **Documentation**: Log exact timing and symptoms in the `Treatment Protocol Log`.

### 2. Equipment Malfunction (PEMF/Hydrogen)
- **Immediate Action**: Press the emergency stop/power down the device.
- **Safety Check**: Ensure the patient is physically safe and disconnected from the device.
- **Technical Lock**: Tag the machine "OUT OF SERVICE" and log the incident in `EQUIPMENT_MAINTENANCE_SCHEDULE.md`.

## 🏢 Operational Emergencies
### 1. Data Breach / System Outage
- **Action**: Immediately disconnect compromised terminals.
- **Notification**: Alert the Technical Lead to isolate the GHL-to-Sheets bridge.
- **Audit**: Review `Agent Activity Log` to determine the scope of the leak.

### 2. Facility Incident (Power/Water/Fire)
- **Action**: Follow standard Alberta building evacuation protocols.
- **Patient Safety**: Priority evacuation of patients currently in treatment sessions.

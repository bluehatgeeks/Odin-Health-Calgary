# Odin Labs Calgary: Daily Opening & Closing Checklist

## 🌅 Opening Procedure (08:00 AM)
- [ ] **Facility**: Unlock main entrance, adjust temperature to 22°C.
- [ ] **Technology**: Power on PEMF and Hydrogen Inhalers; verify status LEDs.
- [ ] **Hygiene**: Sanitize all treatment tables and common areas.
- [ ] **Logistics**: Review today's GHL calendar for patient arrivals.
- [ ] **Inventory**: Check consumable levels (herbal oils, oxygen/hydrogen tanks).

## 🌙 Closing Procedure (06:00 PM)
- [ ] **Patient Logs**: Confirm all `Treatment Protocol Logs` for the day are signed and synced.
- [ ] **Equipment**: Power down medical hardware; perform daily calibration check.
- [ ] **Waste**: Dispose of bio-waste according to Alberta health standards.
- [ ] **Security**: Ensure all patient records are locked/digitized; secure the facility.
- [ ] **Planning**: Sync with Coordinator on tomorrow's priority patients.

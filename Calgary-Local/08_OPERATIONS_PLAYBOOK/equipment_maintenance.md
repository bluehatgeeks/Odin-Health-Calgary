# Odin Labs Calgary: Clinic Equipment Maintenance Schedule

## 🎯 Objective
Ensure the longevity and clinical accuracy of high-value medical hardware through standardized preventive maintenance.

## 🛠 Equipment Matrix

### 1. PEMF Devices
- **Daily**: Visual cable inspection, surface sanitation.
- **Weekly**: Power cycle test, check for electromagnetic interference (EMI) leakage.
- **Monthly**: Full system diagnostic and firmware check.
- **Quarterly**: Professional calibration verification.

### 2. Hydrogen Inhalers
- **Daily**: Reservoir water level check, filter integrity check.
- **Weekly**: Deep clean of inhalation nozzles, check for leaks.
- **Monthly**: Replace primary filters, verify gas purity levels.
- **Quarterly**: Valve and pump pressure test.

### 3. Clinical Workstations (Tablets/Laptops)
- **Daily**: Sanitize screens and keyboards.
- **Weekly**: OS and GHL app updates.
- **Monthly**: Cache clearance and backup of local patient notes to Google Drive.

# Framing the funnel as an explicit two-step process (assessment → clinician call) and embedding the booking widget directly on the results page will convert real leads who currently land on results and go idle

**Funnel:** owen-quiz-funnel
**Status:** Running
**Date started:** 2026-08-19 (deployed to GHL; leads/appointments counted from this date. Same ad set and lookalike audience from the [2026-08-06 test](2026-08-06-owen-qualified-audience-no-gate.md) continues running unchanged — no audience/targeting/budget change bundled with this test, isolating the results-page redesign as the only variable.)
**Date concluded:**

## The change

Three coordinated changes to `owen.html`, all implemented and locally verified 2026-08-17:

1. **Pre-quiz hero states the two-step process explicitly.** New callout box under the intro copy, before Q1: *"How this works: Step 1 — take the assessment, get your pattern. Step 2 — a free call with a clinician to interpret your specific results and map next steps."* Previously the hero only promised "find what's blocking you" / "see your results" — no mention that a call was part of the process at all.
2. **Results page restructured around two explicit steps, not one flat page.**
   - **Step 1 — View Your Results:** collapsed by default (`hidden` + toggle button, `aria-expanded` state). Contains the existing outcome title, intro, 3 pattern bullets, and teaser copy — now reframed with an added line: *"This pattern needs a clinician to interpret it."* Optional — available to whoever wants to read it, but not the default view.
   - **Step 2 — Book Your Free Clinician Interpretation of Results:** expanded by default, immediately visible below the collapsed Step 1 toggle. New CTA copy: *"Your assessment identifies a pattern — not a plan. Book your free 15-minute call and a clinician will walk you through what it actually means and the protocol we'd build around it."* Followed directly by the booking widget itself.
3. **Booking widget embedded as an iframe directly on the results page**, not a link-out. Lazy-loaded (`data-src` swapped to `src` only once the outcome section actually renders, so it doesn't block initial page paint) with an 8-second failure timeout — if the iframe doesn't fire its `load` event in time, or fires an `error` event, a fallback message + the original direct booking link appears in its place so no visitor is ever left looking at a dead embed with no way forward.

New Mixpanel events added: `Outcome Shown` (fires when results render, records which outcome bucket + confirms step2 was expanded by default), `Booking Iframe Loaded`, `Booking Iframe Failed`, `Step1 Results Expanded` (fires only if someone opens the collapsed section). `Booking Link Clicked` still fires but now only means the iframe fallback link was used, not the primary path — tagged with `{ via: 'iframe_fallback' }` so it's distinguishable from the old meaning in historical data.

## Why

Session-replay review (documented in-conversation, 2026-08-17, not yet its own dated test file) of real ad-attributed leads from the [2026-08-06 qualified-audience test](2026-08-06-owen-qualified-audience-no-gate.md) showed a consistent pattern: real leads were completing the full 10-question quiz, submitting their email (Lead event fires), and landing on the results page — then the session simply stopped. No scroll, no click, no `Booking Link Clicked`, no `Schedule`. One traced session showed the results page rendering (pattern bullets, teaser block visible) and then 18+ seconds of complete inactivity before the recording ended. This ruled out both of the two live hypotheses from that test's diagnosis: the booking link itself works (confirmed both by direct manual click and by observing a separate visitor's mouse moving across a fully-rendered, clickable calendar widget without clicking anything), and it's not a `target="_blank"`/webview navigation failure (the click event that would prove that never even fired). The failure is upstream of the click — people are not being moved to act by the page between "results shown" and "CTA."

Diagnosed root cause: the funnel's entire stated promise through the hero and Q1–Q10 is "get your results" — the call is introduced for the first time, unannounced, on the page that's supposed to be the payoff. That's a message-match break at the exact moment conversion is being asked for.

## Decision rule (locked before launch — do not edit after data starts coming in)

Written 2026-08-19, at launch. The goal of this test is unchanged from the funnel's original goal: **booked appointments**, not just leads or clicks — this test exists because the prior design produced leads and zero bookings.

- **Primary metric:** Lead → booked appointment rate. Source of truth is `Schedule` (the server-side GHL event fired on an actual booking) cross-checked against GHL's calendar/CRM directly, since that's unambiguous regardless of which client-side path (iframe success or fallback link) got someone there. `Outcome Shown` → `Schedule` is the funnel view; `Booking Iframe Loaded` and `Booking Link Clicked {via: 'iframe_fallback'}` are diagnostic only, not pass/fail metrics, since a successful iframe interaction may never fire a distinct client-side "clicked" event at all.
- **What Mixpanel actually sees on an in-iframe-completed booking is still unconfirmed** — real-device testing (open item below) will settle this. Until then, GHL's own booking record is the fallback source of truth for whether an appointment was actually booked, so the rule doesn't depend on that open question to be usable.
- **Baseline:** the 2026-08-06 test's real numbers — 9-10 leads, 0% booking-link-click, 0 bookings, Aug 10–17. Any booked appointment at all in this window is already a change in kind, not just degree, against that baseline.
- **Sample size to reach a verdict:** 10 clean leads (excluding self-tests — tag any self-test with a distinguishable UTM or email), counted from 2026-08-19 forward.
- **Pass bar:** ≥10% of leads book an appointment (≈1 in 10). Set low deliberately — the baseline is 0%, so this is a viability floor, not a target: it asks "does this mechanism produce any bookings at all," not "is this the final number." Revisit upward once real numbers come in.
- **Fail bar:** 0 bookings at n=10 → extend to n=20 before final call (rule-of-three logic, consistent with prior Owen tests) rather than killing the redesign on a small sample given the baseline was also 0.
- **On pass:** the two-step framing + embedded-widget mechanism works — keep running, start optimizing booking rate and no-show rate, and treat the audience/no-gate test this was bundled on top of as validated by extension (though its own decision rule should still be evaluated independently).
- **On fail (0 bookings at n=20):** the problem is deeper than results-page framing — likely candidates to isolate next: the iframe itself is failing silently on mobile/FB-webview traffic (real-device testing would surface this first), or the offer/audience itself isn't converting regardless of page design. Do not bundle another variable on top before isolating which one.

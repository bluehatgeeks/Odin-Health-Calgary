# Local Owen Strategy vs USA Lab Funnel Strategy

## Executive Decision

**Updated recommendation after offer clarification: prioritize local Calgary as the lowest-risk operating base, but the USA lab funnel is now a more viable parallel test than originally scored because lab ordering, HIPAA workflows, and U.S. fulfillment are reportedly already handled through third-party infrastructure.**

The local Calgary strategy remains the better foundation because it is fully compliant in Canada, easier to control, easier to prove, and not dependent on lab ordering. The USA lab funnel has the larger ceiling and is operationally more plausible than first assumed because labs are ordered through a third-party physician consult method via Fullscript, infrastructure is described as HIPAA-compliant, and the coaching offer is positioned as protocol construction and health coaching rather than diagnosis.

The key remaining difference is not "can USA operate?" but **which model should get primary focus and budget first**. Local is still the safer base. USA is the scale test.

The strongest practical path is not either/or:

1. **Run Calgary first** to produce proof, case studies, practitioner confidence, intake systems, and coaching retention data.
2. **Use Emma as the primary acquisition avatar and Owen as the high-LTV expansion/referral avatar.**
3. **Run a controlled USA lab funnel pilot in parallel only if ads, landing pages, consent language, and fulfillment boundaries stay conservative and claim-light.**

## New Information Captured on 2026-06-30

### Local Calgary / Owen Offer

- First paid offer: **$300 CAD per month plus herb/supplement costs**.
- Additional margin: Odin Labs makes margin on **herb and supplement sales**.
- Delivery method: **telehealth plus in-person delivery**, with some mail-based supplement fulfillment.
- Labs: **not included** because Canadian rules do not allow labs to be included in this offer.
- Canadian substitute for labs: **detailed clinical assessment**.
- Existing labs: if a client already has lab results, Odin Labs may review/interpret them internally at the clinic.
- Compliance posture: local Canadian offer is described as **fully compliant with insurance, licenses, and scope of work**.

### USA Lab Funnel to Coaching Offer

- Lab ordering: labs are ordered through a **third-party physician consult method with Fullscript**, described as legally allowed without Odin Labs needing additional licenses.
- Result interpretation: lab results are interpreted by a **certified health coach** and used to construct personalized protocols.
- Boundary: results are **not used to diagnose**.
- State targeting: Odin Labs believes it can operate in **all U.S. states** with no specific license barrier under the current model.
- HIPAA/privacy readiness: intake, lab results, email/SMS, video calls, and records are described as **HIPAA-compliant**.
- Coaching price: **$300 per month**, same as Canada.
- Coaching delivery: continuous touchpoints and coaching throughout the month.
- Guarantee: no health outcome guarantee because health outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
- FTC/FDA/state telehealth/lab advertising review: no formal review has been completed because Odin Labs does not intend to make claims.

### Compliance Caveat

The "no claims" posture lowers risk, but it does not eliminate advertising review risk. In health marketing, regulators and ad platforms may evaluate the **net impression** of the funnel, including implied claims, visuals, testimonials, before/after framing, lab-marker language, and protocol promises. This study should therefore treat the USA funnel as **compliance-plausible but still claim-sensitive**, not automatically risk-free.

## The Two Strategies

### Strategy A: Local Calgary Strategy Using Owen

Local clinic-led strategy for affluent Calgary men, especially energy-sector professionals, executives, and business owners. Owen is positioned around performance decline, metabolic drift, cortisol/testosterone optimization, body composition, sleep, and private high-status health optimization.

Core funnel:

`Local search / referral / LinkedIn / private network -> Owen landing page -> discovery call -> intake/labs/assessment -> protocol -> coaching and/or clinic services`

Likely entry offers:

- Free or paid performance/metabolic discovery call.
- Functional testing package where clinically appropriate.
- High-ticket monthly coaching or hybrid clinic plus telehealth support.
- Adjacent clinic modalities such as PEMF, hydrogen inhalation, Ayurveda, and Marma where relevant.

Best acquisition sources:

- Emma referral path: women who get results bring husbands/partners.
- Google search for local intent: testosterone Calgary, low energy men Calgary, functional medicine men Calgary, metabolic health Calgary.
- LinkedIn targeting Calgary executives and owners.
- Partnerships with premium gyms, golf clubs, physiotherapists, chiropractors, and executive networks.

### Strategy B: USA Online Lab Funnel to Coaching

Paid-media-first strategy marketed in the United States. Lab testing is the front-end offer, then buyers ascend into coaching via interpretation, protocol design, and follow-up support.

Core funnel:

`Meta educational video ads -> lab-specific landing page -> lab purchase or lab lead -> results interpretation -> coaching consult -> coaching program`

Current proposed lab ladder:

- Metabolic Foundation Test around $245-$295.
- Micronutrient Panel around $375-$450.
- Thyroid and autoimmune panel around $350+.
- Gut health / stool test around $425+.
- Complete workup around $1,250.
- Coaching ascension around $997-$4,997 or recurring monthly coaching.

Best acquisition source:

- Meta 3-level system: cold educational videos, warm nurture, conversion retargeting.

## Why Local Owen Wins Near Term

### 1. Trust Is the Bottleneck, Not Traffic

Owen does not behave like a broad cold-funnel buyer. The avatar file makes this clear: he decides quickly, values discretion, wants proof, and resists anything that feels like wellness fluff. A local clinic presence gives him signals that a national lab funnel cannot easily create:

- A real Calgary location.
- A practitioner he can meet.
- Local relevance to energy-sector and executive life.
- Private referral paths from spouse, peers, gyms, golf, and professional networks.
- Credible accountability after the sale.

For Owen, the offer should feel like **executive metabolic performance medicine**, not a generic online lab package.

### 2. Calgary Proof Is the Asset That Makes Everything Else Work

The USA strategy needs proof before it can scale efficiently. Local creates proof faster:

- Video testimonials from recognizable local archetypes.
- Case studies around fatigue, body composition, sleep, glucose, cortisol, testosterone, and performance.
- Before/after metrics from coaching and lab markers.
- Practitioner stories that can become educational ad content.
- Referral flywheel through Emma and Owen households.

Without proof, USA paid traffic will likely require aggressive claims, discounts, or inflated projections to convert. That is risky in health advertising.

### 3. Local Has More Ways to Win

Local strategy has multiple acquisition paths:

- Google high-intent search.
- Google Business Profile.
- Local partnerships.
- Referrals.
- LinkedIn.
- Existing clinic modalities.
- In-person events.
- Retargeting.

The USA lab funnel, as currently described, is mostly Meta-dependent. That can work, but Meta account health, policy review, CPMs, and creative fatigue become existential constraints.

### 4. Compliance Is Dramatically Simpler Locally

The USA model is not just "online coaching." It appears to involve lab ordering, test interpretation, health advice, and possibly condition-specific claims. That moves it toward regulated healthcare territory.

Key external constraints:

- The Federation of State Medical Boards says physicians generally must be licensed or authorized where the patient is located, and that the practice of medicine occurs where the patient is located during telemedicine.
- HHS states the COVID-era HIPAA telehealth enforcement discretion expired in 2023, meaning normal HIPAA compliance expectations apply.
- FDA's 2024 LDT final rule was vacated in 2025, then FDA reverted the regulation text, but lab-test oversight remains a moving regulatory area.
- FTC health advertising guidance requires competent scientific support, clear disclosures, and warns that disclaimers cannot rescue a misleading net impression.

This does not make the USA strategy impossible. Based on the new information, the main USA risk is less about lab access and more about advertising net impression, coaching/medical boundaries, documentation, and scale discipline.

### 5. Owen Has High LTV Without Needing National Scale

Owen's economics can be attractive locally:

- Low price sensitivity if the offer is credible.
- Strong ROI framing around performance and productivity.
- Willingness to buy premium private services.
- Potential for long-term monthly coaching.
- Additional household expansion through Emma/Owen pairings.

The local ceiling is lower than the USA market, but the path to a profitable boutique clinic can be shorter and more controllable.

## Why USA Lab Funnel Could Still Become Better Later

The USA lab funnel becomes more attractive if these conditions are true:

1. A licensed clinical partner or compliant ordering model is in place for the target states.
2. Lab vendors support the business model, including ordering, result delivery, and interpretation boundaries.
3. HIPAA-compliant tech stack, consent, privacy, records, and patient communications are configured.
4. Health claims are reviewed and constrained to substantiated, compliant language.
5. Meta ads can run without policy issues for at least 30-60 days.
6. Front-end lab conversion, lab-to-call conversion, and call-to-coaching close rates are measured with real data.
7. Fulfillment can handle asynchronous questions, abnormal results, escalation, and out-of-scope cases.
8. Coaching retention proves that lab buyers ascend and stay.

If those are solved, USA becomes the better scale play because it has:

- Larger market.
- Better paid media data.
- Cleaner value ladder.
- Easier geographic expansion.
- More scalable coaching fulfillment.
- Higher long-term revenue ceiling.

Offer:

Channels:

### Secondary Test: USA Lab Funnel Controlled Pilot

Because lab ordering and HIPAA infrastructure are reportedly in place, USA can be tested sooner than initially recommended. The pilot should still remain constrained until claims, ads, and fulfillment boundaries are validated in practice.

Required before launch:

- Confirm the exact Fullscript / third-party physician ordering flow in writing.
- Confirm who receives results and what language the certified health coach can use.
- Confirm HIPAA vendor coverage and BAAs for every system touching lab results or health records.
- Confirm escalation protocol for abnormal results.
- Prepare ad and landing page language around education, coaching, protocols, and no diagnosis.
- Avoid disease, cure, reversal, guaranteed outcome, or aggressive hormone/thyroid/weight-loss claims.

Once cleared, run a tiny test:

- Target one to three states only.
- Use one entry lab: Metabolic Foundation Test.
- Use conservative educational claims.
- Budget: $20-$50/day for 30 days.
- Goal: validate cost per qualified lab buyer and lab-to-call ascension.

## Financial Model: Practical Interpretation

The current USA model projects strong year-one economics, but it depends on assumptions that must be validated before being treated as forecast:

- Blended CAC around $21.
- Lab-to-coaching conversion in the 40-60% range.
- Warm-audience lab conversion around 8-12%.
- Coaching revenue becoming 70%+ of total revenue.
- Meta ads staying stable.
- Fulfillment capacity keeping up.

Those assumptions are possible in a mature funnel but aggressive for a new health brand without U.S. proof. Treat them as upside scenario, not base case.

For local, the model is less spreadsheet-scalable but more believable:

- A small number of high-ticket clients can create meaningful monthly cash flow.
- Referrals and local search can reduce CAC over time.
- Proof assets compound.
- Clinic services can monetize non-coaching demand.
- The business can learn from real patients before national expansion.

## Final Strategy Choice

Choose **local Calgary first** if the goal is to build a durable, trusted, proof-rich health business with the lowest downside risk.

Choose **USA lab funnel as a parallel pilot** if the goal is to validate national scale while keeping ad spend and claims conservative.

Best decision:

**Local Calgary is the operating foundation. USA lab funnel is now a legitimate controlled scale test, not merely a future option.**

The local strategy should not be "Owen only." The stronger local business is:

- Emma as the highest-probability paid acquisition avatar.
- Owen as the highest-LTV referral, search, LinkedIn, and partnership avatar.
- Calgary proof as the asset that later powers broader telehealth expansion.

### How These Answers Could Change the Recommendation

The recommendation shifts more strongly toward **local Calgary first** if:

- USA lab ordering or interpretation requires a licensed partner that is not yet secured.
- HIPAA/vendor/BAA readiness is incomplete.
- U.S. state targeting is broad or undefined.
- The USA offer depends on aggressive hormone, thyroid, weight loss, or disease-related claims.
- Local delivery is already compliant, insured, and operational.

The recommendation could shift toward **USA lab funnel sooner** if:

- A compliant physician/lab ordering network is already in place.
- The offer is clearly structured as education/coaching with reviewed boundaries.
- HIPAA-compliant systems and insurance are active.
- The front-end lab margin and coaching ascension are validated with real data.
- Local clinic capacity is too constrained to absorb demand.

## 🌿 Ayurveda Vendor Sourcing (Western Canada/Wholesale)
Based on initial research, here are the top leads for bulk Ayurveda herbs and supplies:

### Top Tier Suppliers
1. **Saral Herbs** ([saralherbs.ca](https://saralherbs.ca/))
   - **Key Advantage:** cGMP-compliant manufacturing *within* Canada.
   - **Specialty:** Raw Ayurvedic herbs (Ashwagandha, Triphala, Brahmi).
   - **Verdict:** Best for high-quality, certified raw materials.

2. **The Ayurveda Shop** ([ayurvedashop.ca](https://ayurvedashop.ca/pages/wholesale))
   - **Location:** Vancouver, BC (West Coast advantage).
   - **Specialty:** Full ecosystem—300+ products including oils, spices, and copper/brass tools.
   - **Verdict:** Best for a "one-stop shop" to stock the clinic with both herbs and equipment.

3. **Alpine Herb Company Inc.** ([alpineherb.ca](https://alpineherb.ca/))
   - **Specialty:** Large-volume bulk dried herbs and botanicals.
   - **Verdict:** Best for high-volume/low-cost raw sourcing.

### Other Notables
- **Ayumantra.co**: Strong Canada/US presence for bulk herbs.
- **Apotheka Herbal**: Organic dry herbs sourced from the Pacific Northwest.

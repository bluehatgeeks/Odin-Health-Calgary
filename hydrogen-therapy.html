H₂ — Molecular Hydrogen

Smallest therapeutic molecule in existence

01

Rapid Systemic Distribution

Inhaled H₂ enters the bloodstream within seconds, permeating cell membranes and reaching mitochondria in ~1 minute. No carrier or receptor required.

02

Selective Radical Scavenging

H₂ reacts only with hydroxyl radicals (•OH) and peroxynitrite (ONOO⁻), converting them to water — leaving beneficial ROS signaling untouched.

03

Nrf2 Pathway Activation

H₂ triggers nuclear translocation of Nrf2, upregulating SOD, catalase, glutathione peroxidase, and heme oxygenase-1 — your endogenous defense network.

04

Anti-Inflammatory Gene Expression

Nrf2 activation simultaneously suppresses NF-κB — creating a coordinated cytoprotective response at the gene expression level.

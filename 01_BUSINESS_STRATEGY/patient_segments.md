# Odin Labs Calgary: Patient Segment Framework
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-04

## 👥 Patient Personas & Flow Logic

### 1. The Ayurveda Patient
- **Objective:** Comprehensive systemic correction via Ayurvedic principles.
- **Core Process:** Liability $\rightarrow$ Prakruti $\rightarrow$ Agni $\rightarrow$ Vikruti $\rightarrow$ 30min Plan Design $\rightarrow$ Telehealth Review.
- **Plan Focus:** Constitution, digestive fire, imbalanced state, and herbal/lifestyle correction.
- **Revenue Model:** Initial Consult ($300) $\rightarrow$ Follow-ups ($100).

### 2. The Functional Health Coaching Patient
- **Objective:** Long-term metabolic stabilization and habit architecture.
- **Core Process:** Liability $\rightarrow$ Intake $\rightarrow$ 30min Plan Design $\rightarrow$ Telehealth Review $\rightarrow$ Monthly Coaching.
- **Plan Focus:** Goal setting, dietary frameworks, habit tracking, and functional health milestones.
- **Revenue Model:** First month payment ($300) $\rightarrow$ Monthly subscription.

### 3. The Treatment Patient (PEMF / Inhalation)
- **Objective:** Targeted physiological recovery or symptom relief using advanced tech.
- **Core Process:** Liability $\rightarrow$ Treatment Setup $\rightarrow$ Session $\rightarrow$ Immediate Feedback.
- **Plan Focus:** Frequency settings, duration, and session-by-session progress tracking.
- **Revenue Model:** Per-session fee ($60 per treatment).

## 🛠️ Template Strategy
To maintain efficiency, I will develop three distinct versions of the "Metabolic Roadmap":
- **Ayurvedic Roadmap:** Deep dive into Doshas and Agni.
- **Coaching Roadmap:** High-level goals and habit-based milestones.
- **Treatment Log:** Data-driven tracking of PEMF/Hydrogen efficacy.

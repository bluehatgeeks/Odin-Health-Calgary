## Facts and where they come from (all checked Oct 15 2026 via WebFetch / WebSearch of primary regulator pages)
- Health Canada public advisory RA-81874, original published date 2026-04-09, title "Think twice before injecting peptides bought online: unauthorized products can seriously harm you" (https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/think-twice-injecting-peptides-bought-online-unauthorized-products-can-seriously-harm): peptides generally regulated as prescription drugs in Canada; unauthorized injectable peptides marketed for anti-aging/bodybuilding/wellness are illegal and not assessed for safety/efficacy/quality; "For Research Use Only – Not for Human Consumption" labelling does not make products legal; authorized Rx drugs carry an 8-digit DIN; examples of seized unauthorized products include BPC-157, CJC-1295, DSIP, Epitalon, GHK-Cu, HCG, Ipamorelin, KPV, Melanotan I and II, MOTS-C, NAD+, SS-31, TB-500, Retatrutide; risks include hormonal imbalance, mood swings, blood sugar imbalance, liver/kidney damage, blood clots, tumour growth, infections, contaminants, wrong/missing ingredients; working with CBSA to stop unauthorized shipments.
- Health Canada "Using bodybuilding products" page (https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/topics/buying-using-drug-health-products-safely/safe-use-bodybuilding-products.html): injectable peptides regulated as prescription drugs; research-only labelling does not legalize; similar product name examples.
- FDA Interim Policy on Compounding Using Bulk Drug Substances Under Section 503A (January 2025) and 503B (January 2025): interim policy for bulk use while bulks lists are developed; Category 1 conditions for certain pre-guidance nominations under 503A; 503B bulk use tied to 503B bulks list or shortage list.
- FDA "Certain Bulk Drug Substances for Use in Compounding that May Present Significant Safety Risks": Category 2 safety-risk substances include GHRP-2, GHRP-6, Ipamorelin acetate (503B), Kisspeptin-10 and others; withdrawn-nomination table notes safety/characterization concerns for BPC-157, CJC-1295, Melanotan II, TB-500 fragment, MOTS-c, Epitalon, etc.
- FDA FD&C Act compounding provisions overview: 503A vs 503B roles and exemptions.
- FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee meeting July 23-24, 2026: discussed BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, Emideltide (DSIP), Semax, Epitalon related bulk substances for possible inclusion on the 503A bulks list. Page states that a committee recommendation is advisory only and does not by itself place a substance on the list or change compounding rules (FDA decides through formal rulemaking). Specific vote tallies are NOT used on the page (secondary sources disagree on some tallies; FDA minutes not relied on here).

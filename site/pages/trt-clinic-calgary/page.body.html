How our Calgary TRT clinic works

Everything happens by telehealth, so you can start from home, work or the truck. First we talk: your symptoms and when they started, your sleep, weight, training, stress, alcohol and medications. Then we test: low testosterone is confirmed with two fasting, early-morning total testosterone tests, with free testosterone and SHBG added when the result is borderline (see when to get checked for low T). Then we plan: a written sleep, weight, stress and nutrition plan aimed at what is lowering your testosterone, check-ins and repeat labs on schedule, and your questions about TRT and its costs ready for your next appointment. Reading Calgary and Alberta lab results

In Alberta, community testosterone tests are reported by Alberta Precision Laboratories (APL). APL lists an adult male range of 8.0 to 35.0 nmol/L for total testosterone and notes that results can be up to a quarter lower later in the day, which is why a morning draw between 7 and 10 a.m. is suggested. APL calculates free testosterone from total testosterone, SHBG and albumin, with an adult male range of 175 to 700 pmol/L. Canadian reports use nmol/L and pmol/L, while US articles and forums mostly quote ng/dL and pg/mL; our testosterone calculator converts between them and works out free and bioavailable T from your own numbers. What TRT costs in Calgary

Most TRT clinics charge about $100 to $300 a month, and that fee does not include the medication. Where a clinic lands depends on how often you are seen, whether extra medication is part of the plan and how much supplement support is included. The testosterone itself is inexpensive in Alberta: the Alberta Drug Benefit List carries generic testosterone cypionate injection as a regular benefit at $34.88 CAD for a 10 mL vial, about $15 a month at 100 mg a week before the pharmacy’s dispensing fee, so a clinic in that range comes to roughly $115 to $315 CAD a month in our own arithmetic. Gels are not a benefit on the list, and the oral capsule’s criteria rule out “androgen decline in the aging male.” The full, sourced breakdown is in TRT cost in Canada vs the USA.

## Compliance / content flags (human review)
1. **Who prescribes.** FAQ 6 and the CTAs say a free call helps you decide where to start and what to ask; "medical testing, diagnosis, prescriptions and treatment, including hormone therapy, are provided by a physician". Same wording as the homepage FAQ. It does NOT say Odin prescribes TRT. Confirm that is accurate before adding any "we can prescribe" language.
2. **Cross-border scope.** "Telehealth for men in Canada and the USA" is unverified licensing-wise (same open flag as the other pages). Label text on TRT is US-FDA-specific; Health Canada labelling/regulation was NOT checked (the page says "Labels outside the USA can differ").
3. **Medical content is educational but specific.** Statements checked: Endocrine Society 2018 (confirm with repeat morning tests; look for the cause; advises against starting TRT in men planning near-term fertility; cautions with untreated severe sleep apnea, elevated hematocrit); TRAVERSE primary result and AF / PE / AKI findings, worded "higher incidence ... in the testosterone group" and "small increases"; FDA Feb 2025 (add TRAVERSE results, remove boxed-warning cardiovascular outcome language, new blood-pressure warning) and FDA June 2026 (label updates requested incl. removing the limitation of use for age-related hypogonadism and revising prostate cancer/BPH safety information; source: fda.gov Testosterone Information page, see /workspace/research-trtse/fda.txt). A clinician should sign off. The page quotes no percentages or reference ranges.
4. **Unsourced general statements (worded as association):** short/broken sleep, extra waist weight, heavy alcohol, under-eating, opioids/corticosteroids and anabolic steroids can lower testosterone; sleep apnea goes with low testosterone; TRT side-effect list (acne, fluid retention, breast tenderness, raised red cell count). These are standard guideline items; add citations if desired.
5. **"andropause"** is used as the lay term for the audience; the page itself says "low testosterone" / "hypogonadism" for clinical statements.
6. **Cardiac red-flag line in FAQ 5** ("chest pain, shortness of breath or sudden weakness, seek urgent care") is a safety nudge, not a disclaimer block; remove if Alex wants zero safety wording.
7. **Author / reviewer:** Article schema names the Organization only (no medical reviewer). Add a named, credentialed reviewer if one exists.
8. **/owen-lowt** is deliberately not linked. `sitemap.xml` (live and local) still lists /owen-lowt: check whether that page is [Paid].

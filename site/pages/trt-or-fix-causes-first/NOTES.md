3. **Medical content is educational but specific.** Statements checked: Endocrine Society 2018 (confirm with repeat morning tests; look for the cause; advises against starting TRT in men planning near-term fertility; cautions with untreated severe sleep apnea, elevated hematocrit); TRAVERSE primary result and AF / PE / AKI findings, worded "higher incidence ... in the testosterone group" and "small increases"; FDA Feb 2025 (add TRAVERSE results, remove boxed-warning cardiovascular outcome language, new blood-pressure warning, kept "limitation of use" for age-related hypogonadism).

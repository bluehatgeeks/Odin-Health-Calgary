## Facts and where they come from (all checked Oct 15 2026 via PubMed E-utilities abstracts / Crossref / Endocrine Society press release)
- Corona G, Sparano C, Romeo M, Rastrelli G. Andrology 2026;14:1988-2001 (doi 10.1111/andr.70238; online 14 Oct 2026): 8 GLP-1 RA studies, 375 subjects (overweight/obese, with or without DM); only GLP-1 RAs significantly raised total + calculated free T and gonadotropins; T findings independent of weight loss (suggests direct HPT effect); TRT better than GLP-1 RA for orgasm/sexual satisfaction (not used on page).
- Salvio G et al. Andrology 2025;13:2022-2034 (10.1111/andr.70022): 7 studies, n=680; significant rise in total T (SMD 1.39), FT, SHBG, LH, FSH; meta-regression: T rise related to percent weight/BMI change (page: larger weight loss went with larger rise). Authors: limits don't allow showing a direct testicular action.
- Deameh MG et al. J Sex Med 2026;23 (10.1093/jsxmed/qdaf381): 10 studies, 639 men; total T consistently increased; free T inconsistent, often offset by SHBG rise; LH/FSH preserved or increased vs suppression in TRT comparator groups; semen benefits in obese/hypogonadal men (not used except via link to /trt-and-fertility).
- Orra SH et al. BMC Urol 2025;25:311 (10.1186/s12894-025-02005-0): 4 studies, 219 pre-treatment patients; bioavailable T increased; free T and SHBG no significant change.
- La Vignera S, Condorelli R. Pharmaceuticals 2026;19(8):1321 (10.3390/ph19081321): narrative review of 29 studies; mean total T increase 2.5-5.2 nmol/L with GLP-1 RAs and tirzepatide in obesity-related functional hypogonadism, largely mediated by weight loss and insulin-resistance improvement; RCTs n=12-42, 12-24 weeks (page says only the 2.5-5.2 figure and the mediation conclusion).
- La Vignera S et al. Reprod Biol Endocrinol 2025;23:92 (10.1186/s12958-025-01425-9): 83 men (mean age 55.3), 2 months, hypocaloric diet + brisk walking; tirzepatide (2.5 then 5 mg weekly, n=28) vs no drug (n=30) vs transdermal T (n=25); tirzepatide group: higher LH, FSH, SHBG, TT, fT, bioT; lower E2. Lean-mass result NOT used (baseline LM difference between groups).
- Portillo Canales S, Iqbal I, Akande R, Wood E, Albert SG, Dhindsa S. ENDO 2025 abstract MON-708, J Endocr Soc 2025;9 (10.1210/jendso/bvaf149.1941, abstract read via Crossref) + Endocrine Society press release 14 Jul 2025: retrospective chart review, 110 men (mean age 54, 86 with T2DM, incretin ~1.6 yr; semaglutide 68%, dulaglutide 16%, tirzepatide 38%, 27% >1 drug); weight 116 -> 104 kg; total T 322 -> 380 ng/dL, free T 7.0 -> 8.2 ng/dL, SHBG 36 -> 38 nmol/L (p=0.23); weight change correlated with T change (r=-0.30); men with total T >300 ng/dL 53% -> 77%; authors: "likely due to weight loss", "free T measurement is essential in obese men" since weight loss raises SHBG. Conference abstract (not peer reviewed paper).
- Look M et al. Diabetes Obes Metab 2025;27:2720-2729 (10.1111/dom.16275): SURMOUNT-1 DXA substudy n=160 of 2,539 (124 tirzepatide, 36 placebo; 73% female; mean 102.5 kg, BMI 38); change in body weight/fat/lean -21.3%/-33.9%/-10.9% (tirzepatide) vs -5.3%/-8.2%/-2.6% placebo; ~75% fat / 25% lean of weight lost in both arms. SURMOUNT-1 72-week design confirmed on Lilly HCP page.
- Mozaffarian D et al. Obesity Pillars 2025;15:100181 (10.1016/j.obpill.2025.100181) (corrigendum/erratum published 2026; text not rechecked beyond abstract): muscle and bone loss named as a challenge; recommends baseline muscle strength/function/body-composition exam and preserving muscle and bone via resistance training + appropriate diet. No protein gram targets quoted on page (abstract gives none).
- Lundgren JR et al. NEJM 2021;384:1719-1730 (10.1056/NEJMoa2028198): 195 adults after 8-week low-calorie diet, 1-year strategies; combination (exercise + liraglutide) body-fat percentage -3.9 points, ~twice exercise (-1.7) or liraglutide (-1.9).
- Reused from age-or-weight page: Corona 2013 (24 studies, +2.87 diet / +8.73 surgery nmol/L), Friedl 2026 abstract (energy deficit, sleep disruption suppress LH), Cooper 2015 (SHBG 36 vs 50 nmol/L, obese vs non-obese, as stated on the age-or-weight page).
## Review flags
1. Corona 2026 meta-analysis (Andrology, online 14 Oct 2026) is one day old; volume/pages taken from PubMed record (14:1988-2001). Check final citation before relying on it long term.
2. Mechanism is contested (Corona 2026: weight-independent; La Vignera & Condorelli 2026: largely weight-mediated). Page states both and says the reviews agree on direction.
3. All GLP-1 evidence is in men with overweight/obesity (many with T2DM); studies are small/short; Orra/Deameh/Salvio overlap in primary studies, so the men-per-review bar chart is NOT additive (legend says "studies overlap"). No claim for men at normal weight.
4. ENDO 2025 numbers are from a conference abstract/press release; page attributes them as "presented at ENDO 2025". "About 10 percent weight loss" = authors' conclusion and 116 -> 104 kg.
5. No Health Canada / FDA labeling statements are made (not verified; none needed). The page never says Odin prescribes GLP-1 drugs or testosterone.
6. Graphic 'labs' is conceptual (circle counts are not data); it carries no figures.
7. llms.txt header/older lines still carry disclaimer/physician-style wording and an /owen-lowt link (pre-existing; only the new Answers line was added).

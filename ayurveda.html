The Three Doshas

Your Prakriti — your unique combination of the three doshas — determines how your body responds to food, stress, seasons, and lifestyle.

Vata · Air & Space

Vata

Air · Space · Movement

Governs all movement — nerve impulses, circulation, breathing, elimination. When balanced: creative and adaptable. When disturbed: anxiety, dryness, poor sleep.

Imbalance Signs
  • Anxiety & restlessness
  • Dry skin & joints
  • Insomnia
  • Bloating
  • Variable appetite
Protocol Focus Warming foods, grounding routines, Abhyanga oil massage, regulated sleep, nervous system support.
Pitta · Fire & Water

Pitta

Fire · Water · Transformation

Governs metabolism, digestion, and transformation — physical and mental. When balanced: sharp and focused. When disturbed: inflammation, acid reflux, burnout.

Imbalance Signs
  • Inflammation
  • Acid reflux
  • Skin rashes
  • Irritability
  • Excess heat
Protocol Focus Cooling foods, reduced intensity, Sheetali breathing, bitter herbs, anti-inflammatory lifestyle.
Kapha · Earth & Water

Kapha

Earth · Water · Structure

Governs structure, lubrication, and stability — tissues, joints, immunity. When balanced: calm and strong. When disturbed: weight gain, congestion, slow metabolism.

Imbalance Signs
  • Weight gain
  • Congestion
  • Fatigue
  • Slow digestion
  • Fluid retention
Protocol Focus Stimulating foods, movement, dry brushing, pungent herbs, lymphatic support.

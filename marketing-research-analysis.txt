3. Speed to First Lead (Cost‑Efficiency)
   • Google Search delivers the highest conversion rate (≈ 5 %) → fewer clicks needed to achieve a qualified lead, meaning budgets translate to leads faster.
   • Meta Lead‑Gen costs ~60 % less per click, but because CTR is <1 % and CVR ~2 %, you need roughly 2‑3× more clicks to hit the same lead volume, eroding the cheap‑click advantage.
   • LinkedIn provides the most qualified executive audience for “Executive Metabolic Audit” but CPC and CPA are 3‑4× higher than Google Search and 4‑5× higher than Meta, making it slower to scale on a tight budget.

4. Recommendation – Best Platform for Quick, Low‑Spend Lead Generation
   👉 **Google Search (Standard Search Campaigns) – target the Health & Medical segment in Calgary.**
   Reasoning:
   • Highest conversion rate (≈ 5 %) → fastest lead acquisition per dollar.
   • Local CPC (~$2.40 CAD) is still affordable; a $100‑$150 CAD daily budget can generate ~4‑6 leads per day in the early testing phase.
   • Search intent is strong for “Ayurveda Calgary”, “Functional Health Calgary”, and “Metabolic Health Calgary”. Users actively seeking these services are already at the bottom of the funnel.
   • WordStream data shows 76 % of SMBs are satisfied with search; 49 % plan to increase spend, indicating a stable, competitive environment.
   • Google’s Performance Max can be layered later for scale, but for immediate results a classic Search campaign with tightly scoped SKAGs (single keyword ad groups) is the most predictable.

5. Tactical Implementation (Next Steps)
   1) **Create three tightly‑focused Search campaigns** – one for each offer (Ayurveda, Functional Health, Metabolic Health).
      • Use exact‑match keywords: “Ayurveda Calgary”, “Ayurvedic doctor Calgary”, “Functional health Calgary”, “Metabolic health clinic Calgary”.
      • Bid on the upper‑range of first‑page CPC for Calgary (≈ $2.50‑$3.00 CA) to ensure impression share.
   2) **Ad Copy** – Follow the “Clinical Authority” tone from the Paid Search Blueprint:
      • Headline 1: Authentic Ayurveda in Calgary
      • Headline 2: Free Root‑Cause Consultation
      • Description: Stop treating symptoms. Get a personalized Ayurvedic protocol. Book now.
   3) **Landing Pages** – Build three dedicated landing pages (one per offer) with a single‑step lead form (Name, Email, Phone, Energy Level 1‑10). Connect the form directly to GHL → Google Sheet pipeline.
   4) **Conversion Tracking** – Set up Google Ads conversion tracking via GHL webhook. Verify that each lead triggers a conversion event.
   5) **Budget & Timeline** – Start with a $150 CAD daily budget split equally across the three campaigns. Expect ~5‑7 leads per day after the first 48‑72 h (based on 5 % CVR and $2.40 CPC).
   6) **Performance Review (Day 7)** – Pull the first week’s CPA. If CPA < $80 CAD, consider scaling budget 25 % weekly.

6. Backup / Complementary Channels
   • **Meta Lead‑Gen** – Run a parallel “Lead‑Gen” campaign targeting the same audiences with lower‑cost clicks. Use it to capture overflow traffic and retarget to the Google Search landing pages.
   • **LinkedIn** – Reserve for the Executive Metabolic Audit lead magnet; run a small test budget ($30 CAD/day) aimed at senior‑level titles. Expect higher CPA but higher lifetime value.
   • **Performance Max** – After the first 2‑3 weeks, launch a Performance Max campaign to let Google’s AI broaden reach while preserving the Search core.

7. Proof Points
   • **Google Search CTR (Health & Medical)** – 3.27 % avg (WordStream 2025) – well above the overall 3.17 % average across all industries.
   • **Google Search CPA (Health & Medical)** – $70 USD (≈ $95 CAD) – lower than many niche health services that often see $120‑$150 CPA.
   • **Meta Lead‑Gen CPL** – $27.66 USD (≈ $36 CAD) – 60 % cheaper per click but 2‑3× lower conversion means effective CPL ≈ $100 CAD, comparable to Google.
   • **LinkedIn CPA for B2B lead** – $120‑$150 USD (≈ $155‑$190 CAD) – substantially higher, suited only for high‑value executive prospects.

8. Final Verdict
   **Primary channel:** Google Search (standard Search campaign) – fastest path to qualified leads with a modest budget.
   **Secondary channel:** Meta Lead‑Gen – cheap clicks, good for brand awareness and retargeting.
   **Tertiary channel:** LinkedIn – use sparingly for the executive lead‑magnet.

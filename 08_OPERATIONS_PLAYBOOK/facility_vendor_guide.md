# Odin Labs Calgary: Facility Setup & Vendor Coordination Guide

## 🎯 Objective
Provide a standardized framework for coordinating with vendors and managing the physical setup of the Calgary clinic.

## 📦 Procurement Workflow
- **Step 1: Approval**: Await sign-off on `CONSUMABLES_FINAL_SKU.md`.
- **Step 2: Order Placement**: execute P0 orders via approved vendors.
- **Step 3: Logistics Tracking**: Log all shipment IDs and expected delivery dates in the `Odin_Labs_Clinic_Dashboard` (Inventory Tab).
- **Step 4: Receipt & QA**: Verify items against the SKU list upon arrival.

## 🤝 Vendor Management
- **Primary Contact**: maintain a centralized directory of vendor account managers.
- **Payment Terms**: ensure all net-30 or upfront payments are synced with financial planning.
- **Return Policy**: document return windows for medical hardware (PEMF, Hydrogen Inhalers).

## 🏗 Facility Layout (High-Level)
- **Treatment Zone**: Ergonomic placement of PEMF and Hydrogen equipment for easy patient transition.
- **Practitioner Zone**: Quiet space for Ayurvedic assessments and roadmap design.
- **Intake Zone**: Digital-first registration area utilizing GHL tablets.

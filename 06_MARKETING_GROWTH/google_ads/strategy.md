# Odin Labs Calgary: Paid Search Strategy (V2 - Data Driven)

## 🎯 Strategic Objective
Capture high-intent leads in the Calgary market by bifurcating the approach between "Direct Demand" (Ayurveda) and "Category Interception" (General Natural Health/Naturopathy).

## 🛠 Campaign Architecture

### Campaign 1: The "Direct Intent" Engine (Ayurveda Only)
- **Goal:** Maximum Conversion Rate (CVR).
- **Target Audience:** Users who already know, trust, and are specifically seeking Ayurvedic medicine.
- **Keyword Strategy:**
    - `Ayurveda Calgary` (Vol: 170/mo)
    - `Ayurvedic Doctor Calgary` (Vol: 40/mo)
    - `Panchkarma Treatment Calgary` (Niche/High Intent)
    - `Shirodhara Calgary` (Niche/High Intent)
- **Positioning:** "The Definitive Choice."
    - Focus on authenticity, expertise, and the specific protocols offered at Odin Labs.
    - *Messaging:* "Authentic Ayurveda in Calgary," "Root-Cause Healing via Vedic Tradition."

### Campaign 2: The "Market Intercept" Engine (General Natural Health)
- **Goal:** Market Share Expansion & Brand Awareness.
- **Target Audience:** Users dissatisfied with conventional medicine, searching for "holistic" or "natural" alternatives, often using "Naturopath" as a proxy term.
- **Keyword Strategy:**
    - `Naturopath Calgary` (Vol: 2,900/mo) - **High Volume Driver**
    - `Functional Medicine Calgary` (Vol: 590/mo)
    - `Integrative Medicine Calgary` (Vol: 480/mo) - **Efficiency Play (Low Competition)**
    - `Holistic Health Clinic Calgary`
- **Positioning:** "The Superior Alternative."
    - **CRITICAL:** Do not pretend to be a Naturopathy clinic. Instead, position Ayurveda as the "Next Level" or "Root-Cause" evolution of holistic health.
    - *Messaging:* "Beyond Naturopathy," "The Ancient Root of Functional Medicine," "Why Ayurveda is the Ultimate Holistic Solution."

## 📈 Execution Guidelines
1. **Budget Split:** Recommended 70% Direct / 30% Intercept (until CVR for Intercept is proven).
2. **Landing Page Alignment:**
    - Campaign 1 $\rightarrow$ Direct Ayurveda/Clinic Landing Page.
    - Campaign 2 $\rightarrow$ A "Comparison/Education" Landing Page that explains the difference between standard naturopathy and the Odin Labs Ayurvedic approach.
3. **GHL Pipeline:** All leads flow into the `Odin_Labs_Clinic_Dashboard` via the verified API bridge.

## 🏁 Finalized Execution Parameters
- **Intercept Tone:** Boldly Disruptive. Messaging focused on: "Discover the Root Cause of Your Illness."
- **The Offer (CTA):** Free Root Cause Discovery Consultation.
- **Budgetary Approach:** Prioritizing traffic volume and data gathering over CPL for the initial launch phase. Optimization will follow after baseline data is established.

- **Minimum Daily Test Budget:** Based on the observed average CPC of $1.55 and a benchmark 5 % conversion rate, roughly 20 clicks are needed per expected lead. 20 clicks × $1.55 ≈ $31 CAD per day. A **$30‑$35 CAD daily budget** is therefore the absolute minimum that will likely generate at least one lead per day and give the Smart‑Bidding algorithm enough data to exit the learning phase within a few weeks. Smaller budgets (e.g., $10‑$15) can still run but will produce a lead only every 3‑7 days and will slow optimization considerably.

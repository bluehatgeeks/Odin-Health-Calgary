Prepare for Your Session

While you wait for your results, here's how to get maximum value from your Lab Decode Session.

📝 Start a Symptoms Snapshot

Over the next 7–10 days, jot down your energy patterns, sleep, cravings, mood shifts, and digestive changes.

💊 List Meds & Supplements

Make a simple list of current medications and supplements (name + dose), so your practitioner can put your labs in context.

❓ Note Your Top 3 Questions

Write down the three biggest things you want answered (e.g. fatigue, weight, mood, sleep) so we can prioritise them together.

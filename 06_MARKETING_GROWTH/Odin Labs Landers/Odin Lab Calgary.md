## 3. ⚡ Bioenergetics Therapy

### **Overview**

Bioenergetics therapy focuses on the **Human Body‑Field** — a network of energy and information flows that guide the body’s physical and emotional functions. When these flows become blocked or distorted, health and vitality can decline. The Energy4Life (E4L) approach to bioenergetics aims to detect and correct these distortions, helping restore optimal energy and function.

### **How It Works**

The Energy4Life Bioenergetics Wellness System (BWS) operates through three core steps:

1. **Assessment / Scanning**
   Advanced scanning software evaluates the body‑field to identify areas of energy blockage or informational distortion. This goes beyond symptoms to uncover underlying energetic imbalances.

2. **Rejuvenation / miHealth Therapy**
   The *miHealth* handheld biofeedback device applies gentle micro‑currents and pulsed electromagnetic fields (PEMF) to clear energetic blockages, stimulate cellular repair, and improve energy flow.

3. **Re‑imprint / Infoceuticals**
   Liquid remedies called *Infoceuticals* are imprinted with specific energetic information designed to restore the correct blueprint of the body‑field and support natural self‑healing.

### **Benefits**

* Supports recovery and performance by improving energetic communication throughout the body.
* Enhances vitality, focus, and emotional wellbeing.
* Complements PEMF and hydrogen therapy for a complete energetic‑restoration approach.
* Non‑invasive and designed to work alongside conventional and functional health practices.

### **Evidence and Considerations**

Energy4Life cites user studies showing significant self‑reported improvements after sessions using the *miHealth* device. However, as with most energy‑based systems, these methods are still being researched and are not yet fully recognised in conventional medical frameworks.

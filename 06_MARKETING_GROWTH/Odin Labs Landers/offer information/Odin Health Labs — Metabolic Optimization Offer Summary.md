# 1. Business and Offer Overview

Odin Health Labs provides personalized metabolic health optimization services using a combination of:

- Health coaching
- Functional medicine principles
- Ayurvedic assessment and therapeutic principles
- Personalized supplementation and herbal protocols where appropriate
- Ongoing treatment monitoring and protocol adjustment

The primary service is structured as an **ongoing monthly health optimization membership rather than a fixed-duration program**.

There is deliberately no standard promise that every client will complete treatment within a predetermined period such as 30, 60, or 90 days.

Each client's presentation is different.

Treatment is therefore built around:

1. Identifying the client's primary complaints.
2. Completing a detailed assessment.
3. Developing an individualized protocol.
4. Implementing the protocol.
5. Monitoring the client's response.
6. Adjusting the protocol as needed.
7. Continuing treatment until the client's original concerns have meaningfully improved or the treatment objectives change.

The desired outcome depends on the reason the individual initially sought treatment.

Examples include improvement in:

- Energy
- Mental focus and cognitive performance
- Stubborn weight gain
- Body composition
- Libido
- Hormonal function
- Sleep
- Recovery
- General metabolic function
- Other symptoms identified during the intake

For example, if a client presents primarily because of decreased libido, restoration or meaningful improvement of libido becomes one of the treatment objectives.

# 5. What the Membership Includes

## Comprehensive Initial Intake

Approximate duration:

**90 minutes**

The purpose is to obtain a detailed understanding of the client's:

- Current complaints
- Health history
- Symptom pattern
- Lifestyle
- Diet
- Sleep
- Stress
- Exercise
- Relevant metabolic concerns
- Hormonal concerns
- Existing interventions

For Calgary clients, this intake is conducted **in person at the clinic**.

For virtual clients, it is conducted remotely.

---

# 6. Protocol Design

Following the intake, Odin Health Labs develops an individualized treatment protocol.

The treatment strategy may draw from:

- Health coaching
- Functional medicine
- Ayurvedic medicine
- Nutrition
- Lifestyle modification
- Supplementation
- Herbal interventions
- Other appropriate supportive strategies

The exact protocol depends on the individual client's presentation.

Treatment is therefore not based on a standardized one-size-fits-all program.

---

# 7. Protocol Review

After the protocol has been created, the client receives a dedicated approximately:

**30-minute protocol review appointment.**

The purpose is to:

- Explain the findings
- Explain the treatment strategy
- Review the recommended interventions
- Explain why individual components are being used
- Answer client questions
- Establish the implementation plan

For Calgary clients, this appointment will generally be conducted by **Zoom**, because another physical clinic visit is not necessary.

---

# 8. Continuous Communication Model

One of the primary structural differences between Odin Health Labs and a conventional episodic appointment model is the amount of contact available between scheduled appointments.

The client is not simply given a plan and then asked to return several weeks later.

Instead, an ongoing communication channel is established.

Possible communication platforms include:

- SMS
- WhatsApp
- Another mutually convenient messaging platform

## Minimum Contact

The minimum intended cadence is approximately:

**One check-in per week.**

## Higher-Intensity Monitoring

When a protocol is being actively modified, communication can occur considerably more frequently.

For example:

- Daily communication during medication or protocol adjustment
- Client questions throughout the week
- Discussion of symptoms as they change
- Adjustments based on client response
- Troubleshooting problems with implementation

The client therefore has access to the practitioner throughout the month rather than only during formal appointments.

---

# 9. What Is Not Included in the $300 Membership

The $300 monthly membership pays for Odin Health Labs professional services.

It does **not** include the cost of products or interventions recommended as part of treatment.

Additional client expenses may therefore include:

- Supplements
- Ayurvedic herbs
- Other herbal products
- Medications
- Laboratory testing
- Other therapeutic products or interventions

These costs are separate from the monthly membership.

# 13. Stage 3: Free Consultation

The consultation is free.

It functions as a brief preliminary assessment rather than the full clinical intake.

During this conversation, the practitioner:

1. Asks what prompted the individual to seek help.
2. Identifies the major complaints.
3. Obtains enough preliminary information to understand the general presentation.
4. Discusses the possibility that a natural and individualized treatment approach may help address the problem.
5. Explains Odin Health Labs' ongoing health coaching and metabolic optimization service.

Examples of concerns raised during the consultation may include:

- "I can't focus."
- "I'm tired all the time."
- "I'm working out but I can't lose weight."
- "The weight around my stomach won't move."
- "My libido isn't what it used to be."
- "I don't feel as sharp as I used to."

The objective of this call is to determine whether the Odin Health Labs service appears appropriate and, if so, invite the person to become a client.

# 36. Important Characteristics of the Odin Health Labs Model

The defining characteristics of the service as currently designed are:

### Personalized

There is no standardized protocol applied to every client.

### Presentation Driven

Treatment goals are based on the client's actual problems.

### Ongoing

Treatment is not restricted to a predetermined 30-, 60-, or 90-day program.

### High Contact

The client has access throughout the month rather than only during appointments.

### Adaptive

Protocols can change as the client's presentation changes.

### Integrative

The methodology combines health coaching, functional medicine principles, and Ayurveda.

### Membership Based

Revenue is generated through an ongoing $300 monthly professional-services membership.

### Treatment Costs Are Separate

The membership pays for professional services and support rather than bundling every supplement, herb, medication, test, or treatment product into the monthly price.

BUDGET ALLOCATION: THE 80/20 SPLIT
Original thinking (everyone does this): Spend 100% on conversion campaigns targeting "ready to buy now" people.
Problem: 10:1 ratio means you're fighting over 10% of market (Today Buyers). Everyone competes for same people. Costs spike. ROAS drops.
Rapid Branding approach (20% improvement over time):
80% of budget → Direct sales (conversion campaigns, today buyers)
20% of budget → Branding (building warm audience, tomorrow buyers)
Why this works:
·           Tomorrow Buyers (20% reach) cost $2-5 per person to reach
·           Today Buyers (80% focus) cost $35-50+ per person to reach
·           As your warm audience grows, conversions get cheaper
·           By Month 6, you have 5,000+ warm people who convert at 10-15% instead of 2-3%

Clinical Research

Backed by Peer-Reviewed Science

Over 2,000 published studies support PEMF therapy. Here are key findings from leading medical journals.

Rheumatology · 2016

"Pulsed electromagnetic fields in knee osteoarthritis: a double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized clinical trial"

Patients receiving PEMF therapy showed a 57% reduction in pain scores (VAS) and significant improvement in function compared to placebo.

Bagnato GL, et al. — Rheumatology (Oxford), 55(4):755–762

Clinical Orthopaedics · 1977

"A non-operative salvage of surgically-resistant pseudarthroses and non-unions by pulsing electromagnetic fields"

Groundbreaking research leading to FDA clearance of PEMF in 1979 for non-union bone fractures — with a healing success rate exceeding 80%.

Bassett CA, et al. — Clin Orthop Relat Res, (124):128–143

J Rehab Medicine · 2009

"Effectiveness of PEMF therapy in the management of osteoarthritis of the knee: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials"

Meta-analysis of multiple RCTs confirmed statistically significant pain relief and functional improvement with PEMF, with no serious adverse effects reported.

Vavken P, et al. — J Rehabil Med, 41(6):406–411

Bioelectricity · 2019

"The use of pulsed electromagnetic field to modulate inflammation and improve tissue regeneration: A review"

PEMF was found to down-regulate pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β) while promoting tissue repair — supporting its use in post-surgical and chronic inflammatory conditions.

Ross CL, et al. — Bioelectricity, 1(4):247–259

      Metabolism Is Not Just About Weight.

      Metabolism is how your body produces energy.

      Every heartbeat.Every thought.Every hormone signal.Every repair process.

      All of it depends on your ability to convert food and oxygen into usable cellular energy.

      When metabolism is stable, you feel:

       

      • Clear-headed
      • Steady in your mood
      • Physically capable
      • Rested after sleep
      • Resilient under stress

      When metabolism becomes unstable, symptoms begin to appear.

      Not all at once. Gradually.

      • Energy drops.
      • Weight resists.
      • Sleep lightens.
      • Hormones fluctuate.
      • Stress tolerance shrinks.

      These are not isolated problems.

      They are downstream effects of metabolic dysregulation.
